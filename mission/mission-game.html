About the Game

Journey of the Snowland is an educational adventure game inspired by the lived experiences of Tibetan youth in exile. Through storytelling, puzzles, and interactive tasks, players step into the shoes of a young character navigating life in a Tibetan settlement—visiting key places like the school, monastery, community center, and university.

Each location offers a mission that blends cultural knowledge with modern skills. Whether it’s answering a wisdom quiz in the monastery, creating a digital poster for advocacy, or helping out in the community, players discover what it means to grow, learn, and contribute while in exile.

🎮 How to Play

  • Use your arrow keys (← ↑ ↓ →) or WASD to move around the map.
  • Walk into buildings like the school, monastery, university, and community center to unlock story-based tasks.
  • Complete mini-games, quizzes, and creative challenges to earn badges.
  • Once you’ve visited all key buildings, head to the CTA office for a final mission that brings everything together.

This game is designed for students to explore Tibetan identity, civic responsibility, and the power of knowledge—all while having fun.
